Made for engineering courses and engineering work

Thermodynamics, heat transfer, fluid mechanics and energy systems, from the first homework to the design calculation.

Equations in any order

Write the equations as on paper. EQS finds which unknown each equation gives and solves the equations together where they must be.

Units checked

Every number has its unit, and EQS checks each equation. See SI and English units side by side.

Fluid properties

Water, steam, refrigerants, air, gases and mixtures, brines and incompressible liquids, from the open CoolProp library.

Tables and plots

Change one input over a range and see every result. Plot curves, zoom, fit curves, and export to SVG and PNG.

Property diagrams

T-s, P-h, h-s and more, with the states of your cycle drawn on them. The psychrometric chart for humid air.

Cycles as flowsheets

Draw pumps, turbines, heaters and condensers. EQS writes the equations and shows the stream table and the energy balance.

Analysis

Optimisation, uncertainty and sensitivity, and integration in time.

Learn by example

More than 40 worked examples and a built-in guide that teaches EQS step by step.

Questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. EQS runs on your computer, and your models stay in your own files. The Mac App Store version makes no connection of its own.

What can I do without buying?

Open and read every model and example at any time. The free 14-day trial solves without limits. After it, a purchase or a licence file unlocks solving again.

My university has a licence. Does it work on my Mac?

Yes. In EQS, open Help → License Status… → Install License File…, and choose the licence file from your university.

Which fluids and data does EQS have?

The fluids of the open CoolProp library (water and steam, refrigerants, air, gases and their mixtures, brines), humid air, ideal-gas data for combustion, and property tables of common solids.

Where do I get help?

Help → EQS Guide in the program, and the support page.
